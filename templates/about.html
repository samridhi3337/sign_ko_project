About SignBridge

SignBridge is a dual-directional sign-language AI platform combining live camera-based ASL alphabet and word recognition with a 3D avatar that performs real, captured sign-language motion from a 220-word vocabulary dataset for typed text.

Credits & Third-Party Datasets

Word-Level Recognition & Avatar Motion Capture

  • WLASL (Word-Level American Sign Language Video Dataset) — source clips for word recognition and our 220-word avatar animation dataset.
  • Ready Player Me — the avatar's rigged 3D mesh (Feminine.fbx), sourced from Ready Player Me's open-source repository (MIT licensed).
  • Studio Galt Sign Language Mocap Archive — real professional motion-capture ASL clips (Xsens suit + finger-tracking gloves) for a subset of dataset words (CC0-1.0).

Kozha / CWASA (Sign Rendering Path)

  • CWASA (Character/Web Avatar Signing Agent) — 3D signing engine developed by Virtual Humans Group, University of East Anglia.
  • Kozha — open-source text/HamNoSys/SiGML pipeline bundling CWASA (MIT licensed).
  • ASL-LEX 2.0 — sign data source covering 14 ASL signs and A-Z fingerspelling (CC BY-NC 4.0).
